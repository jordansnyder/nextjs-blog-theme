I believe the best technology disappears—it becomes the experience itself. That belief has driven my career across immersive entertainment, emerging platforms, and creative innovation.

Today, I lead teams of creative technologists building next-generation experiences and platforms. I thrive in both the early chaos of R&D and blue-sky exploration, and the disciplined focus required to ship production-quality work. The best outcomes happen when you can bridge those worlds.

Over 25 years, I've had the privilege of working across advertising, design and technology consulting, startups, Fortune 100 companies, immersive art installations, themed entertainment, and film. Each domain taught me something different about craft, scale, and what it takes to move people.

I'm always interested in connecting with others who are building at the edges of what's possible.
